FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Here are some common questions about our platform to help you understand better.

What does this platform actually do?

It brings all your messages from different apps into one place and shows you only what truly needs your attention. It filters, summarizes, and prioritizes everything so you don't feel overwhelmed.

Which platforms does it support?

It can connect with tools like email, Slack, WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, and more. The goal is to handle all your communication channels in one unified system.

How does it decide what's important?

It uses AI to understand context — like who sent the message, what it's about, and how urgent it is. For example, a message from your manager is treated as more important than a promotional email.

Will I miss important messages?

No. Important messages are always highlighted and brought to your attention. Less important ones are still stored, but they won't interrupt your focus.

Can it summarize conversations?

Yes. It can give you short summaries of long email threads or chats so you quickly understand what's going on without reading everything.

Does it reduce notifications?

Yes. Instead of getting constant alerts from every app, you receive only meaningful, prioritized updates at the right time.

Is my data safe and private?

Yes. Your data is securely handled, and the system only accesses what is needed to organize your messages. Privacy and security are a top priority.

Who is this for?

It's for anyone who feels overwhelmed by too many messages — students, freelancers, professionals, or teams managing multiple communication platforms daily.
